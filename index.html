GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We got you.

A few practical details before you install.

Is MOTIONFLOW PRO a standalone application?＋

No. It is a dockable CEP extension panel that runs inside Adobe After Effects and uses an ExtendScript host bridge for native operations.

Which After Effects version is supported?＋

The 0.8.0 package targets After Effects 2025 (25.x) and compatible CEP-based releases. Other versions may work, but are not verified by this build.

Does it change my project destructively?＋

Most operations create or edit native layers and properties inside After Effects undo groups. Some tools skip animated or expression-driven properties for safety. Always save a copy of important projects before trying a new development build.

Does the Glass system blur the background?＋

Yes, it creates masked native Gaussian Blur adjustment layers behind translucent editable surfaces. It needs visible image or footage detail beneath the glass; a flat solid or transparent area has nothing meaningful to blur.

Does it include AI generation, Whisper captions or map data?＋

No. Those are not included in this build. MotionFlow focuses on the implemented native motion-design workflows and does not present unimplemented features as available.

Can I change the accent colour?＋

Yes. Use the Appearance control at the top of the panel to choose a preset accent or your own custom HEX colour. The panel accent is separate from the colour used for project layers.
